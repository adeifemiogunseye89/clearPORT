Supplier Compliance
Portal

Generate a no-login shareable link and send it to your supplier in China, Dubai, or Europe. They upload their draft Invoice and Packing List — ClearAI checks it against your Form M before they send Shipping Instructions to the carrier, while corrections still cost nothing instead of a paid Bill of Lading amendment later.

🔗

Why this eliminates the most expensive document errors

Most invoice mismatches originate with the foreign supplier — not the clearing agent, and not the carrier. No login is required — suppliers open a link and upload, nothing more. Note: the supplier cannot amend the Bill of Lading itself — that's issued by the shipping line. What they can fix for free is their own Invoice and Packing List, and the Shipping Instruction they send the carrier, before that B/L is ever cut.

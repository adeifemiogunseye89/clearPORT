On the Roadmap

Coming Soon

Three features on the way. None are live yet — shown here so you know what's planned, not to imply they work today.

🤖 Cargo Status Chatbot WhatsApp / Telegram bot answering "where is my cargo?" in plain English, 24/7. Needs a real cargo-tracking data source to be honest — not built until that connection exists.
🌦 Disruption Alerts Predicts delays from weather and Lagos traffic before they happen, with route suggestions. Needs live weather/traffic feeds and a prediction model — Python/ML work, not yet started.
🕐 Audit Trail A timestamped record of every document check you've run — useful as evidence for demurrage waiver claims or AEO applications, not as a way to compel an officer to act. Needs a backend to store history reliably; not built while everything else here runs entirely in your browser.

Why Form M comes first — and why the Bill of Lading isn't in this check

Your Form M is filed with your bank before the order is even placed — it's the anchor. The Invoice and Packing List come later, from your supplier, once goods are ready to ship. ClearAI checks whether what the supplier actually invoiced matches what you originally declared. The Bill of Lading is issued afterward by the shipping line, not the supplier — it carries a general cargo description and gross weight only, not a matching HS code, so it's reconciled separately once the carrier drafts it (see the Supplier Portal for that stage).
